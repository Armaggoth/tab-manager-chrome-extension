Tab Manager Settings

Behavior

When enabled, pinned tabs will not be affected by sorting, grouping, moving, or closing actions.

When enabled, the "Remove Duplicates" operation will detect and remove duplicate tabs of the same Google Docs, Sheets, Slides, or Form—even if they're open on different pages or sheets.

Access Mode

Choose what opens when you click the toolbar icon: the side panel, or a smaller popup dropdown with the same actions.
